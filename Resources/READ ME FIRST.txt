HiFi Guard
==========

A menu-bar app for Bluetooth earbuds on a Mac. Needs macOS 15 (Sequoia) or newer.
Tested on Intel Macs. The download also includes an Apple silicon build, which has not been tested yet.


INSTALL

1. Drag "HiFi Guard.app" into your Applications folder.

2. Open it. macOS will refuse the first time because the app isn't from the App Store
   or a registered developer. That's expected.
   Go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway",
   and confirm.

   (Or, in Terminal:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/HiFi Guard.app"
   then open it normally.)

3. An earbud icon appears at the top right of the menu bar. Click it.

4. The first time the app needs to change an app's sound, macOS asks to allow
   "System Audio Recording". Click Allow. Nothing is recorded or saved; that
   permission is how it re-plays each app at the volume you set.

It starts automatically at login. Untick "Open at login" in the panel to stop that.


WHAT IT DOES

- Keep earbuds in high quality
  When any app opens your earbuds' mic, Bluetooth drops them to muffled mono "call
  mode". This keeps calls on the Mac's own mic so the earbuds stay in stereo.

- Keep other audio loud during calls
  During a call, macOS turns every other app down by about 15 dB. This measures that
  dip and cancels it, so music and videos stay at the level you set.

- App volume
  A slider for each app that is playing (0-150%). Click an app's icon to mute it;
  click its percentage to reset it to 100%.

- Output and mic
  Switch output device and mic straight from the panel, without System Settings.
  Choosing the earbuds' mic turns "Keep earbuds in high quality" off, since using
  that mic is what forces call mode.


LIMITS

- The earbuds' own mic always means call quality on a Mac. That's Bluetooth, not
  this app; macOS has no support for the newer LE Audio standard that avoids it.
- Call apps that pick the earbuds' mic by name in their own settings (e.g. Zoom)
  still force call mode. Set them to the Mac's microphone or "Same as System".
- Undoing the call dip was built and tested on macOS 26 with WhatsApp calls. On other
  macOS versions the panel tells you if it can't measure the dip.
